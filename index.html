UCSF Cranial Accelerometry Lab (CAL)

Applications for collection and analysis of headpulse data

Anesthesia Monitor

Real-time EEG monitoring and analysis for macOS

This app takes live streamed data from HP headsets and graphs a frequency waterfall of the signals. Used primarily for intraoperative monitoring and is a useful app to observe your own headpulse during cognitive activities. Allows annotations as well.
